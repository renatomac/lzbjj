Optional biometric facial recognition consent

Lake Zurich Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu offers optional facial recognition for class attendance. With permission, facial images are processed using Amazon Web Services (AWS Rekognition) to create facial templates and compare them during attendance check-in. Facial templates are stored in AWS Rekognition. Face enrollment is not required to attend classes; manual attendance is available.

Draft notice — not yet approved for live biometric enrollment. Proposed purpose: optional attendance check-in. Proposed retention: permanently destroy biometric identifiers when the original purpose has been satisfied or within three years of the last interaction, whichever occurs first, subject to applicable law. AWS Rekognition processes and stores facial templates on behalf of the academy. No biometric enrollment is permitted until the written policy and notice have been legally approved.

You may withdraw biometric permission by contacting academy staff and requesting removal of enrolled facial templates. Staff will record the request and initiate deletion; failures require follow-up. You can always check in manually. Profile photographs and historical enrollment images require separate data review.

As the parent or legal guardian, choose whether to authorize this processing for the child named above. Declining does not affect class eligibility.
